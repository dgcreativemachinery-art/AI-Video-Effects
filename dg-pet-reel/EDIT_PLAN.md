# DG Tech Machinery: PET Recycling & Profit reel, edit plan

**Format:** 9:16, rendered at 2160×3840 (4K), 30 fps, about 1:20.
**Style references:**
- WhatsApp 8.35: cinematic cuts, close-ups, slow push-ins.
- Video-30073: kinetic captions, a different colour and size on each keyword.
- Video-79813: glass carousel cards curving beside the speaker's body, with a small white word under the chin.

## Rules (from the brief)
- Each clip is used **once**. Nothing repeats.
- A B-roll cut every **3–5 s**, with a punch-in or push-in on every shot.
- Glass carousel cards sit **beside or below the face, never over it**.
- Captions move between positions: a glass pill at the top, kinetic words over full-screen B-roll, and a small word under the chin while the presenter is on screen.
- Cut every dead word and pause from the voiceover. Remove gaps over 0.25 s, plus false starts.
- Music: `assets/music/bgm.wav` at about 0.14 under the voice, louder in the gaps.
- Sound effects: see the "Sound effects" column below.
